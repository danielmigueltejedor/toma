Toma app icon (filmcraft.svg, filmcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory, and the web favicon where the project has one).

The icon is a portrait of Georges Méliès cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Méliès portrait (cropped).png", from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Méliès_portrait_(cropped).png).
Status of the source: public domain.
